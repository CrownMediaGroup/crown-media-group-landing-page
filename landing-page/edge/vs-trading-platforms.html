When to choose Kingdom Edge

You don't have time to read 50 news headlines before the open. The Kingdom Edge morning brief replaces that scroll — overnight Asia/Europe summary, futures direction, 3 specific tickers to watch with one-line reasons, earnings calendar, all in ~350 words. Read in 3 minutes with coffee.

Your trading style is "informed swing/intraday" not "real-time scalping." Kingdom Edge is built for traders who make decisions on a 1-hour to 1-day timeframe and want context, not for traders who need millisecond-level scanner data.

You want AI summarization built into the product, not glued on after. Every brief is summarized by Claude AI from public market data and news. You don't need to subscribe to ChatGPT separately and paste articles in for summarization.

You want a built-in human checkpoint on the highest tier. Edge tier ($297/mo) includes a weekly 30-minute strategy call with King — useful for traders who occasionally want to talk through their watchlist or a setup they're seeing, not just consume content.

You'd benefit from an information edge but don't need Bloomberg-grade depth. Kingdom Edge targets the gap between "free Twitter feed" and "$24,000/year Bloomberg terminal." If that's where you live, this is built for you.

When the other tools might be better

TradingView is the right call if charting is your primary workflow. The chart engine is genuinely best-in-class for retail. We are not trying to compete on charting in v1 — pair Kingdom Edge with TradingView and you get a strong stack.

Trade Ideas is the right call if you're an active day trader running real-time scans. The Holly AI assistant on the Premium tier scans 10,000+ symbols with sub-second latency. That's a different product for a different trader. Kingdom Edge's 5-minute scans are calibrated for a less aggressive timeframe.

Benzinga Pro is the right call if you live and die by breaking news. Their squawk-box audio + real-time news terminal is built for traders who need to act on a press release within seconds. Kingdom Edge's brief format works on a calmer timeframe — pre-open, midday, close.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use Kingdom Edge alongside TradingView?

Yes — and that's actually the recommended stack for many subscribers. Read the Kingdom Edge morning brief at 6 AM ET for context, then use TradingView for the actual charting and order placement. The two products solve different problems and don't overlap functionally.

Does Kingdom Edge replace Benzinga Pro?

For a true active news trader, no. Benzinga Pro's squawk + real-time newswire is in a different latency class. Kingdom Edge replaces Benzinga Pro for the trader who reads the morning brief, sets watchlist alerts, and acts on a daily/weekly timeframe rather than reacting to breaking news in seconds.

How is Kingdom Edge cheaper than Trade Ideas and Benzinga Pro?

Two reasons. First, we're not paying for institutional-grade data feeds — we use Polygon.io's professional API which is significantly more affordable than the real-time Level 2 feeds Trade Ideas needs. Second, we use AI for content production rather than a team of human analysts. Lower cost structure passes through to lower retail price.

Is the briefing format proprietary or can I get it elsewhere?

The format itself (overnight + 3 tickers + crypto + calendar) is similar to many morning-briefing services from financial publishers. What's distinct about Kingdom Edge is the daily delivery cadence, the AI summarization (so output is consistent and concise), and the integration with watchlist alerts that fire based on conditions you define.

What about MarketBeat, Seeking Alpha, or Morning Brew?

Those are publishers — they deliver curated content for a general audience. Kingdom Edge is built specifically for active retail traders making intraday or swing decisions, with watchlist tracking and alerts on top of the briefs. Different audience, different intent. You could use both.
