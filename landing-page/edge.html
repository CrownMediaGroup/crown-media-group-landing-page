Three strategies to pick from.

Pre-built, back-tested patterns. You pick one at a time. Reconfigure monthly.

Trend Follow

Classic systematic trend-following on liquid large-caps. Holds positions while the trend is intact; exits on the moving-average flip.

Time horizon: days to weeks · Frequency: 1-5 trades/week

Mean Revert

Buys oversold conditions on quality names; takes profits as price reverts to the moving average. Best in choppy / sideways markets.

Time horizon: hours to days · Frequency: 5-15 trades/week

Breakout

Buys validated breakouts above multi-day resistance with volume confirmation. Cuts losers quickly; lets winners run.

Time horizon: days · Frequency: 3-10 trades/week
