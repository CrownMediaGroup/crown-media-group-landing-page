## How to Set Up Your Google Business Profile the Right Way

Most Columbia SC businesses claim their Google Business Profile but leave it 40% complete. That's enough to show up — but not enough to win. Here's what a fully optimized profile looks like:

- **Complete every section**: business name, address, phone, website, hours, services, attributes
- **Add 10+ photos**: exterior, interior, team, work samples, before/after
- **Post weekly**: Google Business Profile has a posts feature — use it for promotions, events, and updates
- **Collect reviews consistently**: Ask every satisfied customer. Respond to every review, positive and negative.
- **Use the Q&A section**: Answer common questions proactively — this content shows up in search

A Columbia SC restaurant or salon that does all of this will outrank competitors with 3x more reviews just by having a more complete, active profile.

## What Good Social Media Management Actually Looks Like

Posting randomly on Instagram and Facebook isn't a strategy. The Columbia SC businesses seeing real results from social media follow a content system:

1. **3–5 posts per week** across Instagram and Facebook — not 10 one week and 0 the next
2. **Content pillars**: rotate between educational, behind-the-scenes, client results, and promotional posts (never more than 20% promotional)
3. **Short-form video**: Reels and TikTok videos are being distributed for free by the platforms right now — this is the best organic reach available
4. **Consistent engagement**: reply to every comment and DM within 24 hours

## The Biggest Marketing Mistakes Columbia SC Businesses Make

After working with dozens of local businesses, these are the patterns we see repeatedly:

- **Posting on social media when they feel like it** — inconsistency kills algorithmic reach
- **No clear call to action** — every post, ad, and email should tell people exactly what to do next
- **Ignoring Google reviews** — not asking for them, not responding to them, not monitoring them
- **Spending on ads before fixing the website** — sending paid traffic to a broken or unattractive site wastes every dollar
- **Trying to do everything themselves** — marketing takes 10–15 hours per week done well; most business owners don't have that time

Every one of these mistakes is fixable. Most businesses see immediate improvement within 30–60 days of getting their marketing systematized.
