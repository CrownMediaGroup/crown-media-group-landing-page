Why Is Columbia SC's Economy Growing So Fast in 2025?

Columbia isn't the sleepy state capital some people remember from a decade ago. The Midlands region is in the middle of a genuine economic boom, and the numbers tell the story.

Major employers like Prisma Health, BlueCross BlueShield, Amazon's fulfillment operations, and a wave of defense contractors near Fort Jackson have been steadily adding jobs. The housing market in areas like Northeast Columbia, Ballentine, and Lexington has been on fire — which means new residents who need local services, from dentists to restaurants to home repair.

Here's what's driving the growth:

  • Military expansion: Fort Jackson remains the U.S. Army's largest Initial Entry Training installation, and its annual economic impact exceeds $4.4 billion in the region.
  • University spending: USC's student body, faculty, and game-day visitors generate hundreds of millions in local economic activity annually.
  • Infrastructure investment: New road projects, mixed-use developments in the BullStreet District, and downtown revitalization are attracting young professionals.
  • Remote work migration: Columbia's lower cost of living compared to Charlotte, Atlanta, and Raleigh is pulling remote workers who still spend locally.

Every single one of those bullet points represents a person who needs to eat, get their hair cut, hire a contractor, find a gym, visit a doctor, or buy a gift. If your business isn't showing up when they search — and they will search — you're invisible to the fastest-growing segments of the local population.

Most Columbia SC businesses struggle to show up in local search results, and it's not because the demand isn't there. It's because their digital presence hasn't caught up with the opportunity. That's exactly what Crown Media Group's local SEO service is built to fix — making sure you appear first when new residents and visitors search for what you offer.

How Does Fort Jackson Create Marketing Opportunities for Local Businesses?

Think about what happens when a young soldier finishes Basic Combat Training at Fort Jackson. They've been eating in a dining facility for weeks. They have money. They have a weekend pass. And they have a smartphone.

Now multiply that by the thousands of soldiers cycling through every month — plus their families who travel to Columbia for graduation ceremonies, plus the permanent party soldiers and civilian employees stationed there year-round.

The Fort Jackson Customer Profile

These aren't hypothetical customers. They have specific, predictable needs:

  • Restaurants and fast food near Gate 2 and Forest Drive — soldiers on pass want real food, fast
  • Hotels and short-term rentals — families come for graduation weekends and need places to stay
  • Barbershops and salons — military personnel need regular grooming that meets Army standards
  • Car dealerships and mechanics — new soldiers often buy their first car right after training
  • Off-post housing services — real estate agents, apartment complexes, and property managers near Fort Jackson stay busy year-round

Here's the insight most business owners miss: these customers search differently than long-time Columbia residents. A soldier's spouse arriving from Texas doesn't know that Two Notch Road has three great nail salons. They Google "nail salon near Fort Jackson" or "best restaurant near Fort Jackson Columbia SC." If your Google Business Profile isn't optimized for those exact searches, you don't exist to them.

A real-world scenario: Imagine a barbershop owner — let's call him Jerome — operating about two miles from Fort Jackson's main gate. He's been in business for nine years. Loyal local clients. But he noticed his chair had empty hours on weekday afternoons. After optimizing his Google Business Profile with military-relevant keywords, collecting 40+ Google reviews that mentioned "military haircut" and "Fort Jackson," and running a simple $10/day Meta ad targeting a 5-mile radius around the base, Jerome filled those empty slots within six weeks. No gimmicks. Just making sure the people already looking for him could actually find him.

That's the difference between hoping for foot traffic and building a system. Crown Media Group helps Columbia SC businesses build exactly that kind of system — combining local SEO with paid advertising management to capture the customers already searching for you.

What Marketing Opportunities Does the University of South Carolina Create?

USC isn't just a football school (though Williams-Brice on a Saturday creates its own economy). It's a year-round economic engine with layers of marketing opportunity most local businesses never fully tap.

Three Distinct USC Customer Segments

  1. Students (35,000+): They eat out constantly, need services, shop local when given a reason, and live on their phones. They discover businesses almost exclusively through Instagram, TikTok, Google Maps, and word of mouth.

  2. Parents and families: They visit for move-in weekend, Parents Weekend, graduation, and football games. They spend at a higher level — nicer restaurants, hotels, shopping. They plan ahead and search Google before they arrive.

  3. Faculty, staff, and graduate students: These are long-term Columbia residents with steady incomes and established spending habits. They're more likely to become loyal recurring customers.

Each of those segments requires a different marketing message, a different platform, and a different offer. Trying to reach all three with one generic Facebook post is like trying to catch three different fish with the same bait. It doesn't work.

Here's what works right now in 2025:

  • Short-form video on TikTok and Instagram Reels to reach students — show your product, your vibe, your personality in 15 seconds
  • Google Ads targeting "near USC" and "Five Points Columbia" to capture parent searches during peak visit weekends
  • Email sequences for faculty and staff — once they try you, nurture them into regulars with a monthly newsletter featuring specials

If you're a restaurant, salon, boutique, or service provider within a mile of campus and you're not running location-targeted social content, you're giving those customers to someone who is. Crown Media Group's social media management is specifically designed to create that kind of targeted, platform-specific content without you having to shoot a single video yourself.

How Should Columbia SC Businesses Adjust Their Marketing for a Growing Population?

Growth sounds great until you realize you're competing with more businesses for the same attention. Columbia's growth means more customers and more noise. The businesses that win are the ones with systems — not just ideas.

The 5-Step Local Growth Marketing Framework

Here's a framework any Columbia SC business can follow right now:

  1. Audit your Google Business Profile — Is your address, phone number, hours, and category correct? Do you have 50+ reviews with recent dates? Are you posting weekly updates? This is your single most important digital asset for local search.

  2. Claim your neighborhood — Stop marketing to "Columbia SC" broadly. Get specific. Target "Harbison," "Irmo," "Cayce," "West Columbia," "Dutch Fork," or "Northeast Columbia" in your content. Hyper-local keywords have less competition and higher intent.

  3. Build a 90-day content calendar — Plan 3 social media posts per week, 2 blog posts per month, and 1 email newsletter per month. That's enough to build momentum without burning out.

  4. Run one paid campaign per month — Even $300/month on a well-targeted Meta ad or Google Local Services ad will outperform $0 spent on hoping people walk by.

  5. Track everything — If you can't tell me which marketing channel brought your last 10 customers, you're guessing. Set up Google Analytics, track your ad spend vs. revenue, and review monthly.

This isn't theoretical. It's the exact playbook. The challenge is execution — because most business owners are already working 50+ hours a week running their actual business. They don't have time to become a part-time marketing director.

That's the honest truth about DIY marketing. It works if you have the time. Most people don't. And the cost of inconsistency — posting for three weeks, disappearing for two months, then starting over — is worse than not starting at all. That's why businesses across the Midlands partner with Crown Media Group to handle the execution. You run the business. We run the marketing. One flat rate, no guesswork.

What Are the Biggest Marketing Mistakes Columbia SC Businesses Make During Growth Periods?

When the economy is booming and new customers are flowing in, businesses get comfortable. That comfort creates blind spots. Here are the most common ones:

  • Relying on word of mouth alone — Word of mouth is powerful, but it doesn't scale. New residents in Lexington don't have a neighbor to ask yet. They're Googling.
  • Ignoring their website — A website that hasn't been updated since 2019 is actively costing you money. If it's slow, not mobile-friendly, or doesn't have a clear call to action, visitors bounce in seconds. Website and landing page design is one of the highest-ROI investments a local business can make.
  • No email list — Every customer who walks through your door and doesn't get on an email list is a customer you have to pay to reach again. Building an email marketing list turns one-time buyers into lifetime customers.
  • Trying to be everywhere at once — You don't need to be on every platform. You need to be excellent on two. For most Columbia SC businesses, that's Google (search) and one social platform (usually Instagram or Facebook).
  • Waiting until business slows down — The time to invest in marketing is when business is good. That's when you have cash flow to build the systems that sustain you through slow seasons.

Think of it like farming. You plant in the season of abundance so you can harvest when others have nothing. Columbia's economy is in a planting season right now. The businesses that build their marketing infrastructure today will dominate for the next 3–5 years. The ones that wait will wonder what happened.
