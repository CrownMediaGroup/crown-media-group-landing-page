Built: 2026-05-11 · For: past clients of Crown Media Group · Goal: 5 reviews in 30 days

---

## How to use

1. Get the Google review short link (after GBP is verified): looks like `g.page/r/XYZ?review`. Paste into every {{REVIEW_LINK}} placeholder.
2. Send via Resend from `king@crownmediagroup.co`. BCC `king@crownmediagroup.co` to self for audit trail.
3. Personalize the first line of each email with what you actually did for them.
4. Cadence: Email 1 → Day 0. Email 2 → Day 5 (only to non-clickers). Email 3 → Day 12 (only to non-clickers + non-reviewers).

## Rules

- **Never offer anything in exchange for a review.** That violates Google's terms and Kingdom values.
- **Never ask someone to leave a 5-star review.** Ask for an honest one.
- **Never review the agency yourself.** Don't even from a different account.
- **Send from king@crownmediagroup.co** — personal-feel, not a marketing automation tone.
- **One reply = stop the sequence** for that person. No more emails. CLOSER takes over if they reply asking questions.

- 5 reviews ≥4 stars in 30 days unlocks the "highly rated" trust signal in Google Maps.
- 12+ reviews ≥4.7 average within 60 days = ranks above any un-reviewed competitor instantly.
- WARDEN logs results to memory after the 30-day mark.
