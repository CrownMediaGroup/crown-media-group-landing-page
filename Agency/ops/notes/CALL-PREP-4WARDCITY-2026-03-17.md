## 3 Custom Discovery Questions

1. "What does @4wardcity's current content strategy look like — is there a dedicated person managing it, or is it handled as it comes?"
   — Reveals if they have infrastructure or if it's ad-hoc. Most churches = ad-hoc.

2. "Pastor Greene's music reaches a national audience. How much of that audience is being converted into 4Ward City community members locally?"
   — Identifies the biggest gap: national music brand not driving local church growth.

3. "When someone visits Columbia and looks for a faith community on Sunday — how confident are you that 4Ward City shows up in that search?"
   — SEO + discoverability angle. Churches are searchable products. Most aren't optimized.
