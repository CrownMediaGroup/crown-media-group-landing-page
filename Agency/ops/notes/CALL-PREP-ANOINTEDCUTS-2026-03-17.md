## 3 Custom Discovery Questions

1. "You've been serving this community for 21 years. When a new resident moves to 29229, how are they finding you right now?"
   — Forces her to name the gap herself. She'll say "mostly word of mouth" — that's your opening.

2. "You specialize in hair loss solutions — that's not something most salons advertise openly. How many people in Columbia even know you offer that?"
   — Opens the niche angle. Hair loss is a search-driven need — people Google this. She has no Google presence.

3. "If 500 people in the Hard Scrabble area saw your work every week — what does that do for your chair fill rate?"
   — Makes results tangible and local. Ties to her exact reality.
