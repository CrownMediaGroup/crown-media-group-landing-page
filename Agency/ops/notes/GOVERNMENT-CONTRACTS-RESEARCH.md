## 2. SAM.GOV REGISTRATION

**Cost: Free.** Anyone charging you to register is a scam.
**Time:** 2–3 hours to fill out. 10 business days to activate.

### What You Need
- EIN (apply at irs.gov/ein — free, instant)
- Legal name exactly as filed: All Glory to Jesus Global LLC
- Physical Columbia SC 29229 address (no PO Box)
- Business bank account + routing number (government pays via EFT)
- NAICS codes from above
- Brief service description (150 words)

### Steps
1. Create account at **login.gov** (SSO gateway for all federal systems)
2. Go to **sam.gov** → "Register New Entity" → "Do business with U.S. Government"
3. Enter EIN + legal name (must match IRS records exactly — no comma variations)
4. SAM auto-issues a 12-character **UEI** (Unique Entity Identifier) — your permanent contractor ID
5. Complete: Core Data, Assertions (NAICS + size), Reps & Certs (compliance questions), Points of Contact
6. In Reps & Certs: self-certify as small business + check the SDB (Small Disadvantaged Business) box
7. Submit → confirm email within 1 day → full activation in 10 business days
8. **Renew annually** — lapsed registration = cannot receive awards

### Critical Pitfalls
- Name mismatch with IRS kills the application. Verify exact spelling.
- Cannot use PO Box.
- Set calendar reminder for annual renewal.
