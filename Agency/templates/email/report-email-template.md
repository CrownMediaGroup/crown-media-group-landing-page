# Template: Client Report Email
## All Glory to Jesus Global LLC

---

## Purpose

Reusable email template for delivering monthly performance reports to clients. Warm, professional, and Kingdom-focused. Replace all `{placeholders}` with client and report data before sending.

---

## Email Template

```
Subject: Your {Month} Marketing Report Is Ready — {Business Name}

---

Hi {Owner First Name},

I hope this finds you well! Your marketing report for {Month} {Year} is ready, and I wanted to make sure you had a chance to see how things went.

Here's a quick look at this month's highlights:

{Monthly Highlights — 2-3 bullet points from the AI summary}
  • {Highlight 1}
  • {Highlight 2}
  • {Highlight 3}

Your full report is {attached / linked below} and covers everything in detail — including what we're focusing on next month to keep the momentum going.

{Report Link or: "See the attached PDF for your full report."}

As always, it's an honor to serve {Business Name}. If you have any questions or want to hop on a quick call to walk through the numbers together, just reply to this email and we'll find a time.

Grateful for you,

{Your Name}
All Glory to Jesus Global LLC
{Your Phone Number}
{Your Email}

---

P.S. {Optional personal note — e.g., "Praying for a great month ahead for you and your team!"}
```

---

## Variations by Report Type

### Strong Month (metrics up)

Replace the opening paragraph with:
```
What a great month! {Business Name} had some real wins in {Month} and I'm excited to share them with you.
```

### Challenging Month (metrics down)

Replace the opening paragraph with:
```
Thank you for trusting us with your marketing in {Month}. It was a learning month — some things worked well and some didn't, and I want to be completely transparent with you about both.
```

### New Client (first report)

Replace the opening paragraph with:
```
Your first monthly marketing report is ready! This is the baseline we'll build from together, so every number you see here is a starting point — not a ceiling.
```

---

## Sending Guidelines

- Send on the 1st of the month between 8–10am client's local time (higher open rates)
- Always attach the PDF or include a direct link — never send metrics only in the email body
- BCC yourself on every report email for your records
- If a client hasn't opened after 3 days, send a short follow-up:

```
Subject: Re: Your {Month} Marketing Report — {Business Name}

Hi {Owner First Name},

Just wanted to make sure this didn't get buried! Your {Month} report is ready whenever you have a moment to look it over.

Reply anytime if you have questions.

{Your Name}
```
