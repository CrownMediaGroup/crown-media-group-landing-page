## The Business

**Client:** Shatiea
**Business:** Fruit of the Spirit Juices
**Location:** Columbia, SC
**Industry:** Health & Wellness / Faith-Based Food Business
**Model:** Cold-pressed / handcrafted juice products rooted in Galatians 5:22-23
**Audience:** Health-conscious Christian women, 25–45, Columbia SC and surrounding area
**Stage at engagement:** Pre-launch / early brand — no professional identity, no content system, no online presence

---

## The Problem Before Crown Media Group

Shatiea had a real product with a genuine calling behind it — but zero infrastructure to grow it.

- No brand identity (no logo, no colors, no defined voice)
- No social media presence or content strategy
- No e-commerce setup to sell product online
- No marketing assets — nothing to show potential customers or stockists
- No paid ad capability
- No system to consistently produce content

The business was alive in vision but invisible online.

---

## What Crown Media Group Delivered

### Brand Identity
- Custom logo designed and delivered
- Brand colors, fonts, and visual system established
- Account rebrand across social platforms

### Content System
- Instagram content strategy built around 5 pillars:
  - Behind-the-scenes (30%) — the process, the mission
  - Educational (25%) — health benefits, ingredients, scripture connection
  - Client results (25%) — testimonials, community
  - Faith (15%) — scripture, purpose-driven messaging
  - Promotional (5%) — offers, CTAs
- Content batch produced: 5 formats including Reel script, carousel, Facebook community post, Story sequence
- Brand voice locked: faith-driven, warm, bold, clean

### AI Video Ad
- 60-second short-form video script written and produced
- Hook anchored in scripture (Galatians 5:22-23)
- CTA: direct to order / DM

### Social Media Management
- Platform setup and optimization
- Consistent posting cadence established
- Hashtag strategy deployed

### E-Commerce
- Online store setup (in progress)
- Product listings with brand-aligned copy

### Paid Ads
- Meta ad campaign strategy built
- Ad copy and creatives developed
- Campaign ready for launch pending Google Ads billing verification
