How slash commands work

Instead of typing a long explanation every time, you just type a short command. I already know exactly what to do. Think of it like speed dial — instead of dialing 10 digits, you press 1.

/sprint-check → Where am I today? What's done? What's next?
/content-batch → Give me a topic, get 5 content formats instantly
/client-prep → Business name → full discovery call script in 30 seconds
/pipeline → Show me all my leads — who needs follow-up today
/proposal → Business name + tier → ready-to-send proposal
/ad-copy → Product or service → 5 headlines + 3 ad variations
/video-script → Topic → 60-second Reel script with hook and CTA
/save-state → Save everything — done, blocked, next action
/restore-session → Pick up exactly where you left off last time
💡 Pro tip for you, David

You don't even need slash commands most of the time. Just talk to me normally — "write a caption for Shatiea's new juice," or "who do I need to follow up with today?" I already know your business, your clients, your prices, and your goals. Just talk.
